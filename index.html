Noor Technolab is a new IT startup that builds websites, mobile apps, custom software and AI solutions, and provides UI/UX design and digital marketing — everything a business needs to grow online.

You're a new company. Why should I trust you?

Fair question! As a new company, every project is important to us. We offer a free consultation, a clear written quote, weekly progress updates and milestone-based payments — so you only pay as you see real progress.

Do you offer startup-friendly pricing?

Yes. We understand budgets are tight when you're starting out. We suggest the right features for your budget and offer special pricing for our early clients.

How long does a project take?

A simple business website usually takes 1–3 weeks. A mobile app or custom software typically takes 1–4 months, depending on features. We'll give you a clear timeline before starting.

Do you work with clients outside my city?

Yes. We work with clients across India and abroad through calls, video meetings and WhatsApp, with regular updates at every step.

How much does a website or app cost?

It depends on what you need. After a short free call, we send you a clear, itemised quote with no hidden charges.

Will I own the website, app and source code?

Yes. Once the project is paid for, you get full ownership of the source code, designs and all accounts.

Do you provide support after launch?

Absolutely. We offer maintenance, updates and support plans so your website or app keeps running smoothly.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. We're happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement before you share your idea with us.
