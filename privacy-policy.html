Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Noor Technolab ("Noor Technolab", "we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit www.noortechnolab.online (the "Website") or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have. It is written in line with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 of India.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you fill in our contact form, email us, call us or message us on WhatsApp, we may receive:

  • your name, email address and phone number;
  • your company name; and
  • details about your project or enquiry, and any files you choose to share.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type and the pages you visit. This is used only to keep the Website secure and working properly.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data such as financial information, passwords, health data or government ID numbers through this Website. Please do not send such information to us.

2. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss your project.
  • To prepare quotes, proposals and agreements.
  • To provide our services and support if you become our client.
  • To send you updates about your project or information you have asked for.
  • To improve our Website and services.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We will not send you marketing messages unless you have agreed to receive them, and you can opt out at any time.

3. Legal basis and consent

By submitting your details through our Website, email, phone or WhatsApp, you consent to us using that information for the purposes described above. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us (see section 10). Withdrawing consent does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only when needed:

  • Service providers who help us run our business, such as website hosting (Vercel), email and communication tools (for example WhatsApp), only as needed to provide their service to us;
  • Legal reasons, if required by law, court order or government authority, or to protect our rights and safety; and
  • Business changes, if Noor Technolab is merged or transferred, in which case your information will remain protected under this policy.

5. Cookies and third-party content

Our Website does not currently use advertising or tracking cookies. To display the Website, your browser may load fonts from Google Fonts, images from Unsplash and technology icons from jsDelivr. These providers may receive your IP address as part of delivering that content and handle it under their own privacy policies. If we add analytics or similar tools in the future, we will update this policy.

6. How long we keep your information

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and follow up, generally up to 2 years if no project begins. For clients, we keep project and billing records for as long as required by law. After that, we delete or anonymise the information.

7. How we protect your information

We use reasonable security measures — such as secure (HTTPS) connections, access controls and trusted service providers — to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or update inaccurate information;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • withdraw your consent; and
  • raise a complaint about how we handle your data.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Children's privacy

Our Website and services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has shared information with us, please contact us and we will delete it.
